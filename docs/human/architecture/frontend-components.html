Counting rules

Direct render sites

Each <ComponentName …> written in source counts once.

Distinct pages

Pages that render the component, directly or inside another component.

Shared vs file-local

Shared components live in frontend/src/components/; file-local ones are used in one file.

Components

Every shared component in frontend/src/components/, grouped by what it's for. General-purpose pieces come first, then the ones built for a particular kind of data.

General purpose

Building blocks used across the app.

AsyncCard

The frame most data cards sit in, showing loading, error and empty states.

Used on: Every page, inside most other components

TopNav

The bar across the top of every page, with section links and the sync status.

Used on: Every page

SyncStatus

Shows a running sync's progress, or a failed stage, inside the top bar.

Used on: Every page, inside TopNav

Loads its own data: GET /sync/status every 5 seconds

PeriodSelect

The Period dropdown: a custom range, the last 28, 90 or 365 days, or a single year.

Used on: Videos, Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics (inside FilterBar)

Loads its own data: GET /meta/date-range to build the year list

FilterBar

The filter card above a page's data. Each page picks its fields: Period with Start and End, Title, Type, Privacy, and any fields of its own. Its Type dropdown is ContentTypeSelect.

Used on: Videos (inside VideoTable), Playlists, Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics, and every Comments tab (inside CommentsTab)

Tabs

A row of tab buttons, used for a page's main tabs and for the Traffic Sources sub-tabs.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics, Sync

Pagination

The Previous, "Page x of y" and Next row under a paged list.

Used on: Videos (inside VideoTable), Playlists, PlaylistAnalytics (inside VideoTable), Sync, and every Comments tab (inside CommentsTab)

DetailHeader

The header card of a video or playlist: thumbnail, title and a row of stats.

Used on: VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Video lists and stats

Show videos and their numbers.

VideoStatsBar

The stats bar at the top of a page: legacy and new video counts, views and earnings.

Used on: Videos, Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

VideoTable

The video list with its own filter bar, sortable columns and paging.

Used on: Videos, PlaylistAnalytics (Videos tab)

VideoCarouselCard

A card that pages through a row of videos with thumbnails, such as Latest Uploads.

Used on: Home, Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

TopVideosList

The Top Videos table, ranked by views or watch time.

Used on: Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

TopPerformersCard

A short list of the top videos or Shorts from the last 7 days, in the Analytics sidebar.

Used on: Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Charts

Show metrics over time.

AnalyticsChart

The main chart: views, watch time or earnings over time, split into videos and Shorts.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

TrafficSourceChart

The same kind of chart, split by traffic source instead of video type.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

UploadStrip

The row of markers under both charts showing when videos were uploaded. Opening a marker lists the videos.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics, inside both charts

Traffic sources

Show where views came from.

TrafficSourcesTable

A table of traffic sources with views, watch hours and each source's share.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

TrafficSourceTopVideosPanel

For each traffic source, the videos that got the most views from it.

Used on: Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

TrafficSourceDonutCard

A donut chart of the share of views from each traffic source.

Used on: Home

Search insights

Show which YouTube searches found the videos.

SearchTermsDonutCard

A donut chart of the top search terms and their share of views.

Used on: Home, Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

SearchTermVideosDonutCard

Pick a search term from its dropdown to see, as a donut chart, which videos it led viewers to.

Used on: Home, Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Related videos

Show which videos send viewers to each other through YouTube's Related Videos.

RelatedReferrerBreakdownCard

A ranked list of the videos that sent viewers here, with each one's share.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

RelatedDestinationsByReferrerCard

Pick a referring video to see which of this channel's videos it sent viewers to.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Analytics tabs

The Analytics and Traffic Sources tabs of the channel and playlist pages.

AnalyticsTab

The Analytics tab: stats bar, daily chart, Top 10 table and the four sidebar cards.

Used on: Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Loads its own data: stats, uploads, daily analytics, Top 10 and sidebar requests, each time the tab opens

TrafficSourcesTab

The Traffic Sources tab: stats bar, traffic chart, and its four sub-tabs.

Used on: Analytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Loads its own data: stats, uploads and traffic sources when the tab opens; each sub-tab except Sources loads its own requests when it opens

Comments

CommentsTab

The whole Comments tab: its own filters, the comment feed grouped by video, and paging.

Used on: Analytics, VideoAnalytics, PlaylistAnalytics

Loads its own data: GET /comments, /comments/videos/{id} or /comments/playlists/{id}

Sync

SyncStatusBanner

The strip at the top of the Sync page with one card per active or finished stage.

Used on: Sync

DatabaseOverview

The Database card above the Sync page's tabs: a donut of storage by table and a bar per table's row count.

Used on: Sync

Loads its own data: GET /sync/database when the page opens

Components on each page

The components each page renders, laid out by tab. TopNav and its SyncStatus sit above every page, and AsyncCard frames almost every card, so neither is repeated below.
